Hi, I'm Yousuf — welcome to my corner of the internet.

I've spent the last 13+ years building analytics platforms, BI dashboards, and — more
recently — the automation that keeps those systems running without anyone having to
babysit them. This blog is where I'll share some of what I've learned along the way:
lessons from leading analytics teams, notes on building self-healing data pipelines,
and thoughts on where GenAI-assisted engineering (tools like Claude Code) actually
helps versus where it doesn't.

A few things you can expect here:

- **Analytics leadership** — hiring, mentoring, and scaling teams that ship reliable dashboards
- **Automation deep-dives** — how I built pipeline monitoring and escalation systems that page the right person automatically, not everyone
- **Tooling notes** — Snowflake, Tableau, Python, and the occasional rant about flaky ETL jobs

If any of that's useful to you, stick around. New posts go up whenever I have
something worth saying — no fixed schedule, no filler.

Thanks for reading.
